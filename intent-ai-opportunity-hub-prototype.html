About this prototype

What this is

A travel insurance version of the Intent AI prototype. The data is the same as the telco version: telco weblogs + Edge SDK. What changes is who it is for: the goals are an insurer’s, and every predicted behaviour is travel-related.

The idea it tests: a telco’s behavioural data can tell an insurer who is about to travel, and how soon, before they have looked at cover.

How it works

  • Opportunity hub, by moment: next to the usual by-goal view, a map of travel behaviour predicted over the next 30 days, in eight clusters. No new screen.
  • Sub-behaviours: each cluster splits by predicted next event (for example flies within 2 weeks, flies in 3 to 6 weeks).
  • Opportunity: those sub-behaviours become the audience splits, each with a cover offer matched to the timing.
  • Simulate: policies sold with and without the campaign.

Data status

All numbers are synthetic and illustrative. The clusters, tokens, shares, conversion rates and simulation are placeholders. Nothing here is a live model read, and no real telco or insurer data is used.

To settle before this is sold

  • Consent. Using a telco’s customer behaviour to market a third party’s product needs the customers’ consent and the telco’s contract to allow it.
  • Who sends the message. This prototype assumes the telco sends the push to its own base on the insurer’s behalf, so no customer data leaves the telco.
  • Conversion rates. The 2 to 6% purchase rates are assumptions, not benchmarks.

Not in this prototype

  • Data story, opportunity and simulation are mocked for one cluster only (getting ready to travel abroad).
  • Creatives, activation settings and performance tabs.
