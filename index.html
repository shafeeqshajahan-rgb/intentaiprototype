About this prototype

What this is

The Intent AI prototype on synthetic telco data (weblogs + Edge SDK), framed for a large mobile operator in Nigeria. It shows how Intent AI's behavioural predictions sit inside the Opportunity hub, next to the goal-led view people use today.

The idea it tests: the behavioural token model can tell an operator what customers are about to do, so opportunities can start from behaviour, not only from goals.

How it works

  • Opportunity hub, by moment: the base grouped by what people will do next. Each cluster is a moment. No new screen: it is a view of the hub.
  • Sub-clusters: inside a moment, customers split organically into three or four flavours of the next event (for example moves within 6 weeks, shops for home internet). Data-driven, no agent needed; an agent can write the narrative on top.
  • Dots: each dot is a group of customers whose predicted next tokens sit close together. Hover one to see its last five behavioural tokens and the sixth Intent AI predicts. Each token has four slots: category, brand, action, where.
  • Opportunities: autopilot builds one opportunity per actionable sub-cluster. Sub-clusters with no sensible action are watched, not turned into opportunities. Inside an opportunity, audience splits are customer traits.
  • Simulate: home internet sign-ups with and without the campaign.
  • Same opportunities, two views: the opportunities built from moments also appear in By goal, marked Intent AI.

Data status

All numbers are synthetic and illustrative. The moments, tokens, shares, take-up rates and simulation are placeholders. Nothing here is a live model read, and no real customer data is used.

To settle before this is sold

  • Lawful basis and consent. Using browsing and location behaviour for marketing needs a lawful basis under Nigeria's data protection rules and the operator's own policies.
  • Edge SDK coverage. Location tokens depend on how much of the base the Edge SDK reaches.
  • Take-up rates. The 4 to 9% sign-up rates are assumptions, not benchmarks.

Not in this prototype

  • Data story, opportunity and simulation are mocked for one opportunity only (home internet for customers moving within 6 weeks).
  • Creatives, activation settings and performance tabs.
